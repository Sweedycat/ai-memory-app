# Founder financial policy

Mandatory owner directive, recorded 2026-11-17. Applies to development, product,
operations and commercial proposals together with AGENTS.md Rules 04–06, 15 and
17. This is an internal operating policy, not evidence of available funds or
authorization to purchase, connect accounts, publish offers or activate services.

**BOOTSTRAP FIRST — REVENUE FUNDED GROWTH. Earn first. Expand second.**

The company must operate without dependence on loans, external credit,
anticipated subscriptions or pending app-store revenue. Revenue counts as cash
only after actual settlement into the company account. No settlement or cash
balance has been inspected or assumed in this engineering task.

## Before first settlement

- No paid AI API activation or mandatory external AI dependency.
- No usage-based paid external service, paid cloud infrastructure, new paid
  subscription or service purchase.
- No uncontrolled consumption billing or free trial with automatic paid obligations.
- No assumption that store receivables or subscriber counts finance operations.

Deliver useful local memory, notes, reminders, search and deterministic summaries
without company-funded AI. Low operating cost must preserve product quality.
Future integrations may have provider-independent interfaces only when useful;
all externally billed capabilities, live billing, voice and wearable services
remain disabled until separately validated and approved.

## Free-service admission

Before introducing any free service, record dated evidence of commercial usage
rights, privacy/data-processing requirements, free-tier and rate/resource limits,
absence of automatic paid conversion or unexpected charges, and safe behavior
at exhaustion. Unknown terms mean no activation. Denial, quota exhaustion and
outages must not erase data, bypass App Lock, silently upload records or make
essential local features unusable. Do not enter billing credentials to obtain a
trial. Existing connected engineering tools are not authorization for new services.

## Paid-service activation gates

All eight conditions are required before a paid service can be proposed:

1. Actual revenue has reached the company account.
2. Taxes and refund obligations have been considered and reserved.
3. Available operating cash reserves are sufficient.
4. Expected service costs have been calculated with current, verified terms.
5. Sudden user-growth and abuse/exhaustion scenarios have been tested.
6. Hard spending controls are implemented, including a reliable stop before the
   budget is exceeded; alert-only budgets are insufficient.
7. Subscription obligations can still be fulfilled when limits are reached.
8. The owner explicitly approves the specific service, cap, purpose and scope.

Target a **90-day operating reserve** before significant recurring service costs.
Approval is mandatory after settlement too; it is never inferred from successful
tests, subscriber counts or a previous service approval. Do not automatically
activate externally billed AI when subscription counts reach a threshold.

## Exposure worksheet

Registered users R are distinct from monthly active users A and paid users P.
Paid users may be inactive; active users may be free. Do not equate these sets.
For each proposed service record fixed monthly cost F, active ratio a, paid ratio
p, actions per active user q, verified marginal action cost c, storage, transfer,
support, store fees, taxes, refunds and contractual minimums. Separate currencies;
do not count gross subscription sales as available cash.

The table is a **synthetic capacity scenario**, not a forecast, provider quote,
pricing proposal or service activation. Assume a = 20%, p = 5%, q = 30/month.
For illustration P is a registered-user subset independent of A.

| Registered R | Active A | Paid P | Monthly actions A × q | Variable exposure at verified unit cost c |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 | 20 | 5 | 600 | 600 × c |
| 1,000 | 200 | 50 | 6,000 | 6,000 × c |
| 10,000 | 2,000 | 500 | 60,000 | 60,000 × c |
| 100,000 | 20,000 | 5,000 | 600,000 | 600,000 × c |
| 1,000,000 | 200,000 | 50,000 | 6,000,000 | 6,000,000 × c |

At each row also test 100% active, 10× actions per active user, peak concurrency,
malicious/repeated requests, provider outage and quota exhaustion. The combined
100%-active/10×-usage stress is 300 × R actions/month: 30,000; 300,000; 3,000,000;
30,000,000; 300,000,000 respectively. This is modeled exposure, not a completed
load test. Compute stressed monthly burn as F + stressed actions × c + other
operating obligations. Plan the 90-day reserve against that burn after excluding
tax/refund reserves; a documented hard cap must bound actual service exposure.
Unbounded or unpriced exposure blocks the proposal.

Current implemented local functionality introduces **zero external service usage
charges** through this milestone at all five sizes. This does not assert that
distribution, support, existing tools, devices or future operations are free.
No provider pricing, financial records or revenue settlement was verified here.

## Product and commercial boundaries

Free must retain useful core people, notes/search, basic reminders and essential
privacy/security. Plus may eventually offer verified organization, life-management
and validated backup tools. Future Pro AI/voice/proactive/wearable capabilities
remain inactive. Package boundaries and prices are provisional and require owner
approval. Preserve existing subscriber entitlements; do not degrade working
features or impose arbitrary limits on deterministic local answers to force sales.

BYOK is evaluation-only: official provider API access, purpose-specific consent,
separate cost disclosure and secure key handling are required. Never collect
account passwords, extract session tokens or treat consumer subscriptions as API
access. No hidden transmission of memories or required AI account.

Marketing may describe only working features. No paid advertisements, external
marketing subscriptions, unfunded gifts or costly referral rewards. Public content,
live billing and final entitlements need owner approval. Referral fraud controls
come after core reliability. User backup archives remain user-owned storage;
developer-hosted archive storage is not introduced by this policy.

## Activation record required for any future proposal

Record service/purpose, dated primary terms, data sent/consent, commercial rights,
settled-funds confirmation without sensitive bank data, reserved obligations,
five-scale worksheet and stress results, 90-day reserve assessment, enforced caps,
exhaustion fallback, subscription fulfillment, rollback/disable procedure,
independent review where applicable and explicit owner decision. A blank or
incomplete record is disabled status, never implicit approval.

## Founder-approved multi-platform payment addendum

The [payment strategy](payment-strategy.md), supplied and approved by the founder
on 2026-11-17, is mandatory. Prepare Google Play Billing, Apple StoreKit, Patreon
Web and first-party website subscriptions behind a provider-independent model.
No provider, checkout, external link, webhook or billing server is activated.
iyzico, PayTR, Stripe and Paddle are evaluation candidates, not selected vendors.

Fees and channel prices must be configurable and versioned by provider, region,
currency, effective dates and verified eligibility. The supplied reference rates
and example prices are preserved in the strategy document as unverified planning
inputs. Verify current official terms before implementation/commercial activation;
never silently treat these as permanent constants or approved commercial prices.
Founder approval is required for every final price, discount and promotion.

Calculate gross revenue, platform/processing/fixed/FX/international/payout fees,
taxes/VAT, refunds, chargebacks, infrastructure, AI and acquisition costs
separately before net contribution margin. Track settlement dates and spendable
cash separately; receivables remain unavailable. Extend the five-scale worksheet
above by channel and stress case; preserve all eight activation gates and the
90-day operating reserve target. Provider commission savings may fund sustainable
customer discounts only after founder approval and verified regional compliance.

Preserve existing entitlements and core functionality. Prevent accidental duplicate
subscriptions; switching channels never automatically cancels an existing plan.
Client payment claims cannot grant benefits. Receipt ownership, server validation,
signed webhook verification, idempotent event handling, expiry/refund/chargeback
and secure account linking are future acceptance requirements. Do not collect raw
card details. Use approved hosted checkout/tokenization only after authorization.
