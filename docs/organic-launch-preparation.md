# Organic launch preparation

Preparation only, 2026-11-17. No store listing, website, video, social post,
community message, feedback endpoint or referral offer has been published or
activated. Public marketing requires owner approval and verified product/device
evidence. No advertising spend, paid marketing service or tracking is introduced.

## Store and website evidence kit

Maintain separate Turkish and English drafts after the app's corresponding
translations are reviewed. Each short/full description must trace every feature
claim to a working accepted flow. Current positioning: remembering people,
conversation context and follow-ups on your own device. Do not claim functioning
Notebook, cloud backup, full AI, subscriptions, voice or wearables before their
gates pass. Describe App Lock honestly; do not claim all local data is encrypted.

Prepare title/keyword candidates around actual user problems, readable localized
descriptions, an accurate privacy explanation, support/cancellation information,
release notes and an accessible product website outline. Verify current store
requirements and commercial rights at the later submission task; no store policy
or metadata compliance is asserted here.

## Screenshot scenarios

Use only fabricated people, notes, numbers and dates, with a verified candidate
on a known device/profile. Do not capture the owner's existing records.

| Frame | Working scenario to demonstrate | Evidence required before capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People | Find a fabricated person by conversation context | Real search/profile flow, readable full name/context |
| Profile | Read notes and last interaction without Edit | Dates and content match saved synthetic records |
| Commitment | Set and complete a follow-up | Future reminder, private delivered notification, history |
| Privacy | Lock and reopen | Real authentication; no owner records or biometric prompts containing personal details |
| Notebook (future) | Save, reopen and search a synthetic note | Production wiring/key/recovery/device gates accepted first |
| Daily briefing (future) | Today's verified responsibilities | Implemented structured records and grounded summary first |

Use consistent typography, uncluttered framing and both target languages only
after review. Do not manufacture screenshots that imply inactive functionality.
Revalidate screenshots after UI changes; no image generation or stock purchase
is needed for this preparation.

## Short demonstration storyboard

For TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, prepare one shared 20–30 second
storyboard, then adapt pacing/captions per platform at the publishing task:

1. Show a common problem: forgetting a conversation or a promise.
2. Enter a fabricated context/note and a follow-up in the working app.
3. Reopen, search and read the actual saved result.
4. Demonstrate the verified reminder outcome and a clear local/privacy explanation.

Do not create false testimonial or assistant answers. Captions must be readable,
sound optional, and all music/media commercially licensed. The founder's themes
may guide drafts: “Because you don't have to remember everything”, “Your life is
already busy enough”, and “Remember the people and promises that matter”. Final
copy, translations, recordings and publication remain separate approval tasks.

## Community, search and Product Hunt

Prepare a factual maker story, a working demo, known limitations, FAQs and a
feedback route. Choose relevant productivity/privacy communities, read their
current self-promotion rules, and ask moderators where required before posting.
Do not scrape contacts, spam, buy engagement or create fake accounts/reviews.
Prepare Product Hunt assets and a response schedule only after core stability;
do not announce a launch date or post without approval.

Website/search preparation should explain concrete daily use cases and accurately
link privacy/support/release notes. No hosting/domain purchase, indexing service,
analytics or external form is activated. Accessible feedback should be opt-in,
minimal and avoid automatically attaching memories; optional screenshots need
preview/redaction/removal and explicit sharing consent before implementation.

## Satisfaction and referrals

Use existing local bug reporting as the starting point. Prioritize clear errors,
useful onboarding and honest subscription/cancellation descriptions before a
referral program. Measure feedback only through a separately consented, reviewed
route; no hidden tracking. Track fabricated beta-task success and reported issues
without copying personal records into diagnostics.

Evaluate referrals only after core stability, with duplicate/reward/refund-abuse
controls and explicit sustainable funding. No costly rewards or promised gifts;
existing subscribers must not be unfairly excluded. Feature entitlement, pricing
and any gift require owner approval under [financial policy](financial-policy.md).
